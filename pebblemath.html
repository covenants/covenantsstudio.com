Pebble Math Privacy Policy

App: Pebble Math (com.covenantsstudio.pebblemath)

Developer: Covenants Studio LLC

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Summary

Pebble Math is a math-learning app for children from Pre-K to Grade 2 (about ages 4 to 8). It works fully offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information, from children or from adults. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party tracking in the app.

Data stored on your device

To remember progress, the app keeps a small amount of information only on the device, inside the app's own storage:

  • Each child profile you create: a nickname (which can be anything, not necessarily a real name), an age band, a grade and a colour.
  • Learning progress: which skills were practised, scores, stars and review dates.
  • App settings, such as sound, read-aloud, calm mode and lesson length.

This information never leaves the device unless a grown-up chooses to export it (see below). We cannot see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details.
  • No location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • No usage analytics, crash reporting SDKs or advertising SDKs.
  • No voice recordings. Read-aloud uses the speech engine already on the device to speak, and the app never listens.
  • Children never type anything into the app beyond tapping answers.

Permissions

  • Internet: included by the app framework. Pebble Math does not send any data over the internet; all lessons, pictures and sounds are inside the app.
  • The app asks for no other permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

Grown-ups area

Settings, progress reports and data tools sit behind a simple grown-ups check, so a young child does not change them by accident.

Export and deletion

In the grown-ups area, a parent can export all profiles and progress as a JSON file, import a backup, reset progress for one skill or one child, or delete everything. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the device. Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases

Pebble Math is free. If optional extra content is offered in the future, it will be bought through Google Play behind the grown-ups check. Google handles the payment; we never receive card or payment details.

Children

Pebble Math is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and it is consistent with the GDPR and similar laws for children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change ever involved collecting any data, we would update the app's Google Play data safety section first and ask for any consent the law requires.
