Khaiso · Desi Calorie Counter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · App ID com.khaiso.app

In short: Khaiso needs an account so your meal log is saved and synced. When you choose to analyze a food photo online, the photo is sent to an AI service to estimate the food and portion, and Khaiso does not store it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your meals or photos for advertising or profiling.

This policy explains how Khaiso: Desi Calorie Counter ("the App"), published by Covenants Studio LLC ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and sign-in credentials, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase, through Lovable Cloud).
  • Profile and goals: settings you enter, such as your daily calorie goal and preferred units.
  • Meal logs: the foods, portions and nutrition values (calories, protein, carbs, fat) you save.
  • Photo-analysis usage: how many photos you analyze each day, so we can apply the daily limit.
  • Connection data: basic technical data such as IP addresses and timestamps, processed by our hosting providers to operate and protect the service.

Food photos

When you tap Analyze online, your photo is sent to Google Gemini through Lovable's AI gateway to identify the foods and estimate portions. Khaiso does not store the photo. The on-device option analyzes the photo on your phone and does not upload it.

Sharing

We share data only with providers that run the service for us:

  • Lovable Cloud and Supabase, which host account and meal data
  • Google (Gemini), through Lovable's AI gateway, which receives a photo only when you choose Analyze online

We may disclose data when required by law or at your direction. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS/TLS and stored with access controls.

Retention, export and deletion

  • Your data is kept while your account is active.
  • Export your profile and meals from Settings → Export my data (JSON and a spreadsheet file).
  • Delete your account and all your data from Settings → Delete my account, or email support@covenantsstudio.com from your account email. Deleting removes your profile, meal logs, photo-analysis counts and sign-in. Verified email requests are processed within 30 days.

Not medical advice

Calorie and nutrition values in Khaiso are estimates for general wellness, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about medical diets.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
