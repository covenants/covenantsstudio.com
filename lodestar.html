Lodestar Privacy Policy

App: Lodestar: Pomodoro & Focus (Android, package com.covenants.pomodorotimer)
Developer: Covenants Studio LLC, 82 Wendell Ave Ste 100, Pittsfield, MA 01201, USA
Effective date: November 9, 2026

Summary

Lodestar is an offline timer. It does not require an account, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your focus history, task titles or preferences to any server. The app does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

  • Timer sessions and their outcomes (mode, stage, timestamps, duration, optional linked task).
  • Tasks you create (title, tag, estimate, completion state).
  • Preferences (theme, timer lengths, sound and vibration settings).
  • The state needed to restore a running timer.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own backup feature may copy it according to your device backup settings.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No location.
  • No advertising ID.
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting.
  • Nothing you enter in the app is transmitted to us or to third parties.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the running timer and completion alerts.
  • Exact alarms: so a timer ends on time.
  • Full-screen alerts (optional, off by default): to show a completion alert over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to restore a running timer after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for the optional completion vibration.

Denying a permission does not stop the timer from working.

Export and deletion

You can export your session history as a CSV file using Android's file picker; the app does not upload it. You can delete your history in Settings → Your data → Delete all history. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Lodestar is a general productivity app and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect or transmit data (such as cloud sync or in-app purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released.
