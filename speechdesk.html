SpeechDesk Translate · Offline voice translation

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · App ID com.covenants.speechdesk

In short: your voice is processed on your phone and recordings are never saved or uploaded to us. History and notes stay on your device. Google ML Kit, which performs translation, collects diagnostic data as described below.

Developer and contact

SpeechDesk Translate is provided by Covenants Studio LLC, 82 Wendell Ave, Ste 100, Pittsfield, MA 01201, USA. Contact us about privacy or support at support@covenantsstudio.com. This policy applies to the Android app with package name com.covenants.speechdesk.

Speech and translation

The app uses your microphone only after you grant permission and start recording. Microphone audio is processed on your device using Whisper and voice-activity detection. The app does not save microphone recordings or upload your audio or translation text to a developer-operated server. Translation is performed on the device with Google ML Kit. Recognition and translation can work offline after the required models have been downloaded.

Speech playback

The app selects installed Android text-to-speech voices that report that no network connection is required. If a suitable offline voice is unavailable, playback is disabled. Android and your selected speech engine are supplied by their respective providers and are subject to their privacy policies.

History and notes

Recognized text, translations and editable notes are saved in a private database on your device. Translation history remains until you clear it; notes remain until you delete them. App backup and device-transfer backups are disabled in the app configuration. Device security and encryption are managed by Android and your device settings.

Model downloads and network requests

Initial setup downloads approximately 160 MB of speech models from Hugging Face and GitHub. Google supplies additional translation models for selected languages. These providers receive normal network request information, such as your IP address, the requested file and request headers. Model files are verified before use. The app explains setup size and mobile-data costs before allowing downloads.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

Google ML Kit and its supporting services collect diagnostic and usage information. Google describes device and app information, installation-related identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, feature events, errors and selected source/target translation languages. ML Kit Translation also uses Firebase installations and remote configuration for diagnostics. Local processing of your translation text does not mean the SDK makes no network requests. See the Google disclosures linked below for details and applicable retention practices.

Sharing

If you choose to share a note, Android sends the selected note text to the receiving app you choose. That app handles the text under its own privacy practices. SpeechDesk does not automatically publish notes or translations.

Accounts and advertising

This version does not require a SpeechDesk account and has no advertising or payment integration. Third-party diagnostics described above are separate from the local history and notes.

Your choices and deletion

You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. You can clear translation history and delete notes in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes the local database, preferences and downloaded speech models. Covenants Studio does not have remote access to that local database and cannot delete it remotely. For data handled independently by Google or model hosts, consult the linked provider policies or contact those providers.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date will be updated on this page. For questions, email support@covenantsstudio.com.
