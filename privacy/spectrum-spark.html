Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026 · App ID com.covenantsstudio.spectrumspark

In short: Spectrum Spark does not collect any information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything you or your child enter stays on your device.

This policy explains how Spectrum Spark (“the App”), published on Google Play and the App Store by Covenants Studio LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information. It covers the App for Android, iPhone and iPad, version 1.1.0 and later.

What the App stores on your device

The App saves these things on the device so it can work:

  • routines you create and which ones are ticked off today
  • stars and badges
  • settings, such as the chosen voice and reduced motion
  • the optional profile: a name, age, interests, sensory preferences, and an emergency contact name and phone number
  • picture cards you create, including any photos you choose for them
  • a simple activity log used for the Progress page, such as how many routines were completed. It holds no typed text and is limited to the last 120 days

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Photos you pick for a card are resized and stored inside the App; the App does not read the rest of your photo library.

Children

Spectrum Spark is designed for children and their families. Because the App collects nothing, there is no personal information from children for us to use, share or sell. A caregiver check protects Settings, the Profile and card editing, so that a child does not change them by accident. The App has no ads, no external links and no social features.

Backup files

In Settings, under Backup, a parent or caregiver can save everything above to a single file and restore it later. You choose where that file goes, for example your own files or cloud storage. We never receive it. The file is not encrypted and may include your child’s name and photos, so keep it somewhere private. Android’s automatic cloud backup is switched off for this App.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the profile with “Delete local profile” on the Profile page.
  • Delete a picture card from “Manage cards”.
  • To remove everything, uninstall the App or clear its storage in your device settings.

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Speech

Spoken words are produced by the text-to-speech voice built into your device. The App asks for a voice that works offline. The words you tap are not sent to us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version collects any information or adds accounts, we will update this page and the privacy details shown in the app stores before that version is released.
