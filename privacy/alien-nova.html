Alien Nova · Arcade Shooter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · App ID com.novastudio.aliennova

In short: Alien Nova does not collect, upload or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

This policy explains how Alien Nova ("the App"), published on Google Play by Covenants Studio LLC ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • your high score
  • game settings such as sound and difficulty
  • in-game statistics
  • in-game credits and ship upgrades earned by playing

This data is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, or any other personal or usage data.

Third-party services

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. When an internet connection is available, it may load display fonts from Google Fonts. This is a standard web request covered by Google's Privacy Policy, and we receive no data from it.

Permissions

The App requests internet access only to load fonts. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
