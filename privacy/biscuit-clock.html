Biscuit Clock · Dog Focus Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · App ID com.biscuitclock.app

In short: Biscuit Clock has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your timer and settings stay on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat only validating the entitlement. There is nothing about you to sell.

This policy explains how Biscuit Clock (“the App”), published on the App Store and Google Play by Covenants Studio LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to version 1.6 and later.

Information stored on your device

To keep your timer working, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device only: the timer phase and remaining time, your round and biscuit count, your chosen dog and treats, schedule and sound settings, purchase entitlement, and which downloaded dog packs are present. This data is not sent to us. You can remove it with Reset everything in Settings or by deleting the App. Your platform’s normal device backup rules may apply; we do not receive or access those backups.

Purchases

Biscuit Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store or Google Play. The store processes the payment and tells the App whether the pro entitlement is active. We never receive your name, email address or payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this App, product interaction related to purchases, and basic device and app information such as platform, app version and country. This is used only to provide the purchase feature. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Downloading Pro dogs

The four free dogs are inside the App. The twelve Biscuit Pro dogs are downloaded after purchase from our content delivery provider, Cloudflare R2. Like any download, the request includes your IP address and standard technical information. Cloudflare keeps short-lived operational logs for security and reliability. The downloaded files are stored on your device so the App keeps working offline. You can remove them with the App’s storage controls when those are available, or by deleting the App.

Notifications

If you enable the end-of-session reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device and asks for permission when you first start a timer. The content of the notification is generated on the device and is not sent to a server.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or general app usage analytics. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal information for marketing.

Children’s privacy

Biscuit Clock is rated 4+ / Everyone and has no ads, social features or user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account, and restore a purchase from the App’s paywall. Local data can be cleared with Reset everything or by deleting the App. For a purchase-data request, contact us and we will work with RevenueCat and the store to identify the anonymous record.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships, and note material changes in the store release notes.
